About me

Eugenio Gómez Alatorre is director of the Master’s in Digital Economics at Universidad Panamericana. He is also a visiting professor at IPADE Business School, where he was a full-time professor for more than ten years. He holds a PhD in Economics and Business from the Universidad de Navarra, a master’s degree in Economics from El Colegio de México, and a bachelor’s degree in Economics from Universidad Panamericana. He is a biweekly columnist at El Financiero and has specialized in strategy, game theory, and the digital economy.

Program director

Master’s in Digital Economics

The Master’s in Digital Economics at Universidad Panamericana trains professionals who can understand the economic impact of digital technologies and lead business transformation with strategic vision and a human perspective. The program builds the skills to analyze business models, evaluate innovation opportunities, and make evidence-based decisions, combining technical knowledge of emerging technologies with economic and managerial judgment to create value in organizations.

Research

My research spans digital economics and business economics, with a growing focus on the economic implications of artificial intelligence, FinTech, and technological change in Latin America. My empirical work includes field experiments using RCTs on ethics communication in organizations and gender inclusion in senior leadership.

UP Economics Research Center

Teaching

I have taught economics, in English, for more than fifteen years: first as a full-time professor at IPADE Business School and, since 2020, at Universidad Panamericana, where I led the undergraduate Economics program before taking on the direction of the Master's in Digital Economy.
